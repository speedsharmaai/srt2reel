Maya wanted to run a marathon.
She told everyone at the office.
"I'm going to run forty-two kilometres," she said.
Everyone clapped. It already felt like winning.
But the next morning, she stayed in bed.
A month later, someone asked, "How is the training going?"
She had not run even once.
So she started again, and this time she told nobody.
Every morning, one more kilometre.
Six months later, she crossed the finish line.
The lesson is simple.
Do the work first. Talk about it later.
